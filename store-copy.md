# Store Copy Draft

## Short description

A 12–18 minute monochrome psychological game where watching people changes the behavior your surveillance system is trying to measure.

## Description

You are the operator of a civic observation pilot. Five public cameras. One performance target: visible order.

Watch too little and real harm can pass unseen. Watch too long and ordinary people begin adapting to the lens. Stress looks suspicious. Suspicion justifies more attention. The system becomes very good at measuring a city it is also changing.

WATCHED is a compact systems-driven narrative with no dialogue tree and no final morality choice. Your ending is generated from your actual operator record: what you watched, what you flagged, what you ignored, what you prevented, and how much pressure your attention created.

### Features

- One focused 12–18 minute run
- Five public feeds plus a hidden sixth camera
- Persistent NPC behavior shaped by observation pressure
- Genuine incidents mixed with harmless anomalies
- Multiple data-driven endings
- English and Arabic UI
- No gore, no jump scares, no telemetry

## Content note

Themes include surveillance, social pressure, false accusation, authority, and implied petty crime. No graphic violence.
